# My Favorite Hobby: Photography
## Why I love it

Photography allows me to capture *beautiful moments* and **tell stories** without words. I learned photography when I was in middle school.

---
## My Gear
Here's a quick look at what I use:

- **Camera:** Cannon EOS R10
- *Lens:* 50mm f/1.8
- **Editing Software:** Lightroom
- *Tripod:* Manfrotto Compact Action
---
## Tips for Beginners
1. Learn how lighting affects your photos. 
2. Experiment with different angles. 
3. Always **back up your files!**
